Taylor Will

I’ve always been driven by a deep curiosity about how things work. Over the years, I realized that exploring data and creating compelling visuals is one of the most effective ways to truly understand complex ideas, and that discovery continues to fuel my passion for learning.

My journey so far has been a blend of structured learning and self-directed projects. Through online courses, I’ve built a strong foundation in mathematics, coding, and data visualization. But for me, the deepest learning happens when I apply those skills to real-world scenarios. Diving into hands-on projects has allowed me to solidify my knowledge, experiment creatively, and continuously refine my approach.

Outside of my professional pursuits, I love exploring the natural beauty of Vancouver Island with my beautiful wife and our baby boy. Whether it’s backcountry snowboarding in the winter, sea kayaking in the warmer months, or free diving year-round, I’m always up for the next adventure. These experiences remind me there’s so much to discover and appreciate in every aspect of life.
